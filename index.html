Enter X01–X07 using the definitions applied to the development set. The application reports point estimates, 90% and 95% empirical prediction intervals calibrated from grouped out-of-fold residuals, development-label provenance, and the applicability-domain (AD) distance percentile.

Intended domain

Neutral, single-component pure hydrocarbons with fully specified molecular structures. Descriptor definitions must match those used for model development.

The application predicts pure-compound physicochemical properties only. It does not assess fuel-specification compliance, blend performance, sustainability, synthesis, safety, emissions, cost, or engine performance. Confirm candidate properties experimentally.

Interval interpretation

The 90% and 95% empirical prediction intervals were calibrated from molecular-formula-grouped out-of-fold residuals. They are neither Bayesian probabilities nor coverage guarantees for an individual compound.

MODEL CARD

Models, variables, and limitations

Fixed serialized models

Volumetric net heat of combustion (Y02) is not fitted independently. It is calculated exactly as predicted Y01 × predicted Y03 to preserve physical consistency between heat of combustion and density.

Development-label provenance

This release is a provenance-aware hybrid dataset rather than an all-experimental database. Experimental, evaluated/compiled, calculated, correlation-estimated, constructed, and unresolved database values are identified in the record-property provenance table; source links and access notes are provided in the reference registry.

Compiled source conditions are retained for density and flash-point labels; these values must not be interpreted as measurements obtained using one standardized certification method.

Formula-derived values contribute substantially to the development labels for flash point and kinematic viscosity at 40 °C. Interpret these predictions with their empirical prediction intervals and applicability-domain (AD) distance percentile.

Limitations

Y01: Net heat of combustion/lower heating value; not higher heating value or life-cycle carbon intensity.

Y04: Pure-compound solid–liquid phase-transition temperature; not the freezing point of a finished fuel.

Y05: Pure-compound boiling point; not a fuel distillation curve.

Y06: Flash point depends on open-/closed-cup method and measurement conditions.

Y07: Kinematic viscosity at 40 °C; not a low-temperature aviation-fuel viscosity specification.

Applicability-domain (AD) distance percentile: Position in the development-set reference-distance distribution; not a calibrated prediction-error probability.

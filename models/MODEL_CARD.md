# SAF-Predict model card

- Version: 1.1.0
- Model metadata generated: 2026-09-05T08:19:59.719174+00:00
- Intended use: preliminary physicochemical-property screening of neutral,
  single-component, SAF-relevant hydrocarbon molecules from seven documented
  molecular descriptors
- Development data: `data/development.csv`, n = 300, 73 molecular-formula
  groups
- Internal held-out test: `data/held_out_test.csv`, n = 10, 9
  molecular-formula groups; Y02 has 8 retained reference labels
- Test-space overlap: 9/10 records, representing 8/9 unique formulae, share a
  molecular formula with the development set

The held-out set is a structurally stratified internal test from the same data-
curation workflow. It is not independent external, prospective, new-chemical-
space, OOD, or experimental validation. The analysis-design statements in this
card are author-reported metadata; repository hashes do not independently
establish when the held-out reference values were first accessed.

## Inputs and outputs

The model takes X01–X07: molar mass, H/C atomic ratio, aromatic-carbon fraction,
ring count, branching-carbon count, the graph-automorphism descriptor, and the
Kier κ2 shape index. It predicts Y01–Y07: mass-based and volumetric net heat of
combustion, density, solid–liquid phase-transition temperature, boiling point,
flash point, and kinematic viscosity at 40 °C. Definitions and units are stored
in `models/model_card.json` and `data/data_dictionary.csv`.

Model-family selection uses five-fold `GroupKFold` with molecular formula as
the grouping variable and RMSE as the selection criterion. The four compared
families are XGBoost, random forest (RF), support vector regression (SVR), and
an artificial neural network. Hyperparameters, random seeds, software versions,
and the full grouped nested-CV record are available in `training/config.json`,
`models/model_training_summary.csv`, and `artifacts/cv/`.

## Y02 definition

Y02 is generated by a separately fitted random-forest model. It is trained only
on 162 identity-resolved, independently collected development responses in 66
molecular-formula groups. The remaining 138 development labels are missing and
are neither imputed nor reconstructed. Held-out Y02 errors and interval
coverage use only the 8 records with a retained Y02 reference label; the 2
missing held-out labels do not contribute to those statistics.

The Y01 × Y03 product is retained solely as a physical-baseline audit and is
not the deployed Y02 rule. “Independently collected” here means independent of
the current Y01 and Y03 release columns; it does not mean that every Y02 value
has been confirmed as a direct experimental measurement. Identity linkage is
documented, but the exact primary source and measurement conditions remain
unresolved for some legacy Y02 entries. These labels are therefore not
uniformly classified as high-confidence experimental measurements.

## Uncertainty and applicability domain

The browser and Python artifacts report symmetric empirical 90% and 95%
prediction intervals calibrated from molecular-formula-group maxima of nested-
CV out-of-fold absolute residuals. They also report a nearest-neighbour distance
percentile in the seven-descriptor space. The 95th-percentile rule is a high-
distance warning calibrated on the development data, not an externally
validated absolute OOD boundary or a calibrated probability of error.

Version-specific held-out R², RMSE, MAE, molecular-formula-group bootstrap
confidence intervals, interval coverage, record-level residuals, provenance
classes, and distance scores are reported in `validation/`. Values are not
duplicated in this card so that the model card cannot drift from the frozen
evaluation artifacts.

## Limitations and prohibited interpretations

- This is a provenance-aware hybrid dataset, not an all-experimental database.
  Provenance classes do not imply harmonized measurement conditions.
- The seven descriptors do not resolve every constitutional, positional,
  stereochemical, conformational, or crystal-packing effect.
- Y04 is a pure-component solid–liquid phase-transition temperature, not the
  freezing point of a finished fuel blend. Do not use Y04 alone to rank or
  select compounds; experimental confirmation is required.
- Y05 is a pure-component boiling point, not a finished-fuel distillation
  curve. Y07 is defined at 40 °C and is not a low-temperature aviation-fuel
  viscosity specification.
- Density and flash-point labels retain heterogeneous source conditions; consult
  `data/provenance_long.csv` before interpreting individual values.
- Predictions do not establish ASTM conformity, finished-fuel suitability,
  sustainability, synthetic feasibility, safety, cost, emissions, or engine
  performance.

## Artifact traceability

The public Joblib and browser bundles contain seven separately fitted deployment
models. Molecule names, molecular formulae, original development-record
identifiers, and bilingual display metadata are removed from the serialized
public model object; X01–X07 reference vectors remain for applicability-domain
calculations and may be linkable to the separately released dataset.

Current public and private-source artifact identifiers are generated in
`models/model_artifact_manifest.json`; the release-wide content hashes are in
`release_manifest.json` and `checksums.sha256`. These hashes identify file
contents and numerical lineage but do not prove analysis chronology or provide
an independent scientific audit.

Predictions are intended solely for research-stage physicochemical prescreening
within the represented descriptor space and require experimental confirmation.
